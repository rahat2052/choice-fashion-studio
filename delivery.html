Everything you need to know about getting your outfit from our rack to your wardrobe.

How To Order

Ordering is simple

1

Pick your items

Browse the collection, choose your size and colour, and add items to your bag.

2

Send via WhatsApp or call

Your bag turns into a ready-made WhatsApp message — or simply call +880 1618-006268 and read out what you liked.

3

We confirm & deliver

We confirm stock, price and delivery details with you directly, then get your order on its way — or keep it ready for pickup.

Delivery

Delivery information

Home delivery

  • Sunamganj town: home delivery may be available — ask us when you order.
  • Other areas: please call or WhatsApp to arrange the best option.
  • Delivery charge & timing: confirmed per order — we'll always tell you the cost before you pay. Verified details coming soon

In-store pickup

  • Reserve by phone/WhatsApp, collect from Nil Akash Plaza, Priyangon Market.
  • Open every day, 10:00 AM – 12:00 midnight.
  • Try before you take it home — fitting first, payment after.
