Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about our store, orders and policies.

Where exactly is Choice Fashion Studio?
We're at Nil Akash Plaza, Priyangon Market, Sunamganj 3000 — one of the town's main shopping markets. Use Google Maps directions or see the map on our contact page.
What are your opening hours?
Our Google listing shows us open every day, 10:00 AM – 12:00 midnight — Saturday through Friday, with no weekly closing day. On festival eves (like before Eid) hours can extend; call ahead if you're coming late.
How do I place an order online?
Add items to your bag on this website, then tap “Order via WhatsApp” — your selections arrive to us as a ready-made message. Or simply call +880 1618-006268. We confirm stock and price before anything is final.
Do you offer home delivery?
Home delivery within Sunamganj town may be available depending on the order — please call or WhatsApp us to confirm availability, charge and timing for your address. You'll always know the delivery cost before you pay.
Which payment methods do you accept?
Cash is always welcome in-store. Mobile payments (bKash/Nagad) or Cash on Delivery may be possible per order — please confirm by phone before relying on them, as we're still formalising these options.
Can I exchange an item if the size doesn't fit?
Bring the item back unused with tags intact and we'll do our best to exchange it for the right size. For anything defective or incorrect, call us first — we always try to make it right. A full written policy is being published soon.
What kind of clothing do you sell?
We stock clothing for men, women and kids — our men's collection in particular is something customers regularly praise in reviews. The online catalog here is a preview; the racks in-store always have more. Call to ask about a specific item.
Are the products and prices on this website final?
No — the online catalog is currently a preview with sample items and indicative prices while we photograph and upload the real collection. Always confirm the actual price and availability by call or WhatsApp before visiting or ordering.
Do you have a Facebook or Instagram page?
We're working on our official social media presence. For now, the reliable ways to reach us are phone, WhatsApp, this website, and our Google Business Profile. Any future official pages will be linked from this site — beware of lookalike pages until then.
