How To Measure

Quick measuring tips

Chest / Bust

Wrap the tape around the fullest part of your chest, keeping it level under the arms. Breathe normally — don't puff out.

Waist

Measure around your natural waistline — where you normally wear your trousers — with one finger between tape and body.

Length

For tops, measure from the highest shoulder point straight down to where you want the hem to sit.
