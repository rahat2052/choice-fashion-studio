About Choice Fashion Studio

Fashion for the whole family, right here in Sunamganj

Choice Fashion Studio is a clothing store at Nil Akash Plaza, Priyangon Market — one of Sunamganj's busiest shopping spots. We bring together clothing for men, women and kids under one roof, so the whole family can find something they love in a single visit.

Our customers describe the store as clean, well-organised and full of unique items — and they keep mentioning our fair prices and friendly service. That perfect 5.0★ Google rating is something we work hard to protect every single day.

Whether you need a panjabi for Eid, a saree for a wedding, or everyday essentials for the kids — our team will help you find the right fit, the right colour and the right price.
